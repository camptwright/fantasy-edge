# NBA props (2026-10-20, opening night)

Owner request: expand NBA player/game props and fantasy support now that the
2026-27 season has started. This adds NBA to the prop board; NBA fantasy is
pending (see the end).

## What already existed

- **Data in place:** ESPN NBA schedule and scores, hourly box scores (28,673
  player-games for 2025-26), Pinnacle/Bovada/ESPN game lines, and SGO props
  (Bovada and ESPN Bet).
- **SGO limits:** one game per call, the next to tip off, and 60 calls a day
  shared by every sport.

## Added

- **`src/ingest/nba_players.py`** (every 6 h): ESPN team rosters create the
  rookies and new signings that had no Player row, so their props no longer
  park, and they set `current_team_id`. First run: 517 rostered, 82 created.
- **`src/ingest/pinnacle_props.py`** (every 10 min, free): Pinnacle guest-API
  player props for points, rebounds, assists, threes and PRA. First run: 449
  parsed, 415 written. Two names park because of the "Jr." suffix (exact
  names only, by design). NBA only: NFL Pinnacle props already come through
  ParlayAPI, so a second copy would count Pinnacle twice.
- **`src/services/nba_context.py`**: from the archived ESPN summaries, each
  player's team per game, the starters, and DraftKings' pregame total and
  spread (`pickcenter`). Covers 1,316 of 1,320 games; 1,064 have lines.
- **Prop board:** NBA is a third sport with the same no-vig consensus and EV,
  hit rates (L5, L10, season, vs this opponent), and D-vs-Pos by G/F/C.
- **`src/services/nba_prop_engine.py`** (research column):
  - recency-weighted minutes times per-minute rates, shrunk to the position
    group;
  - a fitted multiplier for the total, the spread and the opponent;
  - P(over) from empirical actual/mean ratio quantiles;
  - fitted by `scripts/research_nba_props.py` into `config/nba_prop_model.json`.
- **Frozen record:** each game's engine and market are saved once, at the
  first board build within 90 minutes of tip, to `raw/nba-prop-forecasts`.
  This is the record for grading the engine against the market.

## Walk-forward test

Tuned and fitted on 2025-26 regular-season games before Feb 1; tested on
Feb 1 to the end of the season (10,645 player-games per stat).

- **Half-life and shrinkage:** 10 games and 60 minutes.
- **Error (MAE):** beats the last-10 mean on every stat except threes and
  blocks (ties). The 95% CIs exclude zero, but the gains are small:
  - points 4.73 vs 4.78;
  - rebounds 1.91 vs 1.95;
  - assists 1.39 vs 1.41;
  - PRA 6.25 vs 6.32.
- **Probabilities:** beat the base rate (Brier) at a book-like line.
- **Bias:** under-projects points by about 0.48 per game in the test half,
  which is drift through the season; an intercept did not remove it. Over
  probabilities for points, PRA, P+A and P+R run about 2-3 points low.
- **Not yet tested against real lines:** no 2025-26 prop lines were stored.
  The frozen record starts tonight.

## Known limits

- **Last season's roles only:** early in the season the model misses trades,
  new roles and rookies. On opening night its largest disagreements with
  the market were exactly these (Simons, Giannis in Minnesota, Maluach).
- **Not bound to the injury report:** every rostered player with three or
  more games is projected. Props on players who sit are void anyway.
- **No refit during the season yet.** Planned once enough 2026-27 games are
  final.

## NBA fantasy (pending)

- **Sleeper:** the sync already covers NBA, but the owner's only NBA league
  ("2016 warriors", 2025) is complete and no 2026 league exists yet.
- **ESPN:** the sync is configured for football (`ffl`) only.
- **The decision engine is football-specific:** weekly lineups, QB/RB/WR/TE
  slots. NBA needs daily lineups, games-per-week counts, and points or
  category scoring.
