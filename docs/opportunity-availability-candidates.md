# Opportunity and availability shadow candidates

NFL and college player labs retain the existing baseline and usage-blend forecast.
The additional workload candidate uses at least eight paired stat/usage games,
the most recent twenty observations, and shrinks recent five-game usage toward
historical usage with eight prior-game equivalents. Historical efficiency converts
usage into a stat mean, bounded within one historical population standard deviation
of baseline. Missing usage does not become zero or a guessed proxy.

Availability reuses exact player IDs and event-scoped football evidence. Fresh
event-linked out/doubtful/conflicting evidence can record a shadow abstention.
Missing reports never establish health. No participation probability or numeric
injury discount is invented; estimates remain conditional on playing.

Prospective archives freeze both candidates and availability evidence before
kickoff under a new code-derived version. Existing archives remain intact.
Corrected final stats regrade errors; missing/disputed results remain ungraded.
Workload MAE must be compared with workload_paired_baseline_mae on the same
workload_samples, not the overall baseline cohort. Availability action counts are
descriptive, not evidence of improved prediction accuracy.

Neither experiment changes betting serving or promotes automatically. Reliable
snap/route histories, participation labels, and sufficient prospective outcomes
remain prerequisites for a learned participation model or promotion.
